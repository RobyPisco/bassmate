Quick guide

🎸 Studio

Pick a root note and a scale (or a genre: Blues, Funk, Jazz…) and the fretboard lights up. Tap the dots to hear notes. Switch labels (degrees/notes/solfege), tuning and hand. The metronome sits at the bottom; Harmonization, Formula and a share link on the side.

🎼 Chords

Chord diagrams for 4-string bass. Filter by note and type; tap a card to hear the chord.

🥁 Metro

Metronome with BPM, tap tempo, time signature and subdivision. Advanced mode: groove, drums and speed trainer.

🎯 Quiz

Train your ear and memory: guess the note, the degree, the scale by ear or by its shape. Score, combo and leaderboard.

📋 Grids

Printable blank necks for writing scales and exercises by hand. Choose necks, strings and frets, then 🖨 PDF.

⚙️ Simple / Advanced · Theme · Language

Top right: switch mode (Simple hides advanced controls), theme (auto/dark/light) and language (IT/EN). Bassmate works offline and installs as an app.
